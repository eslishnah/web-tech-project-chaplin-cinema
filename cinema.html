About Dostar Cinema

Dostar Cinema

Dostar Cinema is a cinema in Astana. Its interior has strong green branding, light walls, orange accents and dark ceiling panels. We used these visual details as the main idea for the Assignment 2 colour palette.

The clear class places this paragraph below the floated photo. Our team uses real photos from the cinema as visual references for the design.

General information

The cinema has ticket and snack areas, movie halls and seating spaces for visitors. The project presents this information with simple semantic HTML and CSS layouts.
